
SHOPEASE - SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY

1. Standard Delivery
Estimated delivery is generally 3 to 7 business days
after dispatch, depending on the delivery location.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed.

2. Tracking
Customers can track orders through their ShopEase account
using the order tracking section.

The AI assistant does not have access to live order
tracking information.

3. Delayed Orders
If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery
window, customers should contact support with their
order ID.

4. Delivery Exceptions
Delivery may be delayed because of weather, logistics
disruptions, address issues, or public holidays.

5. Missing Orders
If an order is marked delivered but the customer has not
received it, the customer should contact human support
for investigation.

The AI assistant must not claim an order is delivered
or lost without verified tracking information.
