
SHOPEASE - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

1. Return Eligibility
Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery
for eligible products.

Products must be unused and returned with original
packaging, accessories, and applicable tags.

Some categories, including certain personal care products
and customized products, may not be eligible for return.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Products
Customers who receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect
product should contact support within 48 hours of delivery.

Customers may be asked to provide photographs and the
order ID for verification.

3. Refund Processing
Refund requests are subject to inspection and approval.

Once approved, refunds are generally initiated within
5 to 7 business days.

The time required for the refund to appear in a customer's
account may vary depending on the payment provider.

4. Refund Status
The AI assistant cannot confirm whether an individual
refund has been approved or processed.

Customers should contact human support for their
individual refund status.

5. Return Shipping
Return shipping arrangements depend on the reason for
the return and the product category.

Customers should contact support for return instructions.
