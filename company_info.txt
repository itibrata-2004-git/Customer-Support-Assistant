
SHOPEASE - COMPANY INFORMATION

Company Name: ShopEase
Business: Online shopping and e-commerce.

Customer Support:
Our support team handles customer questions about orders,
payments, shipping, returns, refunds, and product issues.

Support Hours:
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
Support is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Customer Contact:
Customers should use the support ticket system to report
order problems or request assistance from a human agent.

Important:
The AI assistant must not invent order statuses, tracking
numbers, refund approvals, or payment information.
It should direct customers to human support when personal
account information or live order data is required.
